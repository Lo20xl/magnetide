1

Fold the two yokes

Fold 90° down at ±28.5 on an R3 die for the walls, then the two lugs out at ±44.06 on R1.5. One blank per half, mirror image.

Check: 57 mm across the plate, 22.5 mm plate face to lug face. Two Ø3.4 holes per lug, 13 mm apart, at x 40 and x 110. R3 is not optional on 3 mm sheet — a sharp die splits the outside of the bend.
Need: 2 × clamshell-flat-3.0mm.dxf, folded · Then: deburr all edges
2

Load the magnets

48 tiles per tray: 6 along the bore, 4 across the width, 2 layers deep. They drop into the cavity and finish flush with the floor.

Compass check before the epoxy sets. All four rows on a face point the same way; the other tray points the other way. One flipped row stalls a quarter of the bore.
Need: 2 × tray, 96 × N52 tile, epoxy · Drawn as 4 bars = the 48 tiles of one tray · drop in, never press-fit
3

Cap it

Drop the 1 mm cap on the ledge. It sits on the magnets and finishes flush with the steel inner face.

This face is the magnet's road into the yoke. A bead of epoxy at the rim only — never a puddle.
Need: 2 × cap · Result: stack height 19.5 mm per half
4

Slide in the electrodes

Each graphite plate slides into the 1 mm groove from the end. Push until it sits 5 mm inside the end pocket.

Full 14 mm face exposed: 124 × 14 = 1736 mm² per plate. Keep the edges clean — a burr on graphite is a short waiting to happen.
Need: 2 × electrode · Fit: 0.2 mm clearance in the groove
5

Bellmouth and rear block

Two Ø3 pegs each, into the blind holes in the tray end faces. Mouth in, straight end out.

Wire it now: drill through the last 0.4 mm of the lead channel, feed the two wires out, epoxy the hole shut around them.
Need: 1 × bellmouth, 1 × rear block, epoxy · Keep: the outlet straight
6

Close the yoke

Lower tray into the bottom yoke, upper into the top. Bring the halves together so the lug faces meet bare — no paint, no gasket.

This is a stack clamp: the tray rim lands on the steel plate, the mirrored tray butts it at mid-plane, the bolts close the sandwich.
Need: 2 × assembled half · Check: split faces bare metal
7

Bolt it

Eight M3 × 14 through the lugs — two per lug, 13 mm apart. Tighten in a cross pattern, a little at a time, so the halves seat flat instead of rocking.

2 N·m gives roughly 8 kN across the four lug pairs. Run a nut and washer on the underside; thread never protrudes into the bore.
Need: 8 × M3 bolt, nut, washer · Check: no gap at any lug face

Before you power it

  • Both halves flush, 8 bolts seated, no gap at the lug faces
  • Compass: every row on a face points the same way, the two faces oppose
  • Tray floor unbroken over the whole magnet area — hold it up to a light
  • Electrodes fully home, not shorting to steel through a scratch
  • Lead exit drilled, wired, epoxied; bore clear down its whole 124 mm
  • Fuse in the supply line; runs under 2 minutes, ventilated (Cl₂)
